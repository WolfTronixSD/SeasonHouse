# Seasons

The same ceiling rig (winch, 2 LEDs, piezo, MP3 player, WLED) changes character with the season. Pick the season on the control page; it is saved, so the prop wakes up in the same season after a power cut. Try every show first in the [simulator](../simulator/index.html).

Swapping seasons on the hardware means hanging a different character on the line and changing the eye LEDs if you want a different colour. Everything else stays.

## Halloween: Corner Spider

The original. A black spider tucked into the ceiling corner. Red eyes, hiss, scream and lightning.

**Build:** Foam or plush spider under 250 g; Red 5 mm LEDs as eyes; WLED: purple/orange breathe, lightning, blood red.

**Shows:** *The Classic Drop* (lightning, scream, free fall), *Creeping Doom* (heartbeat, creep, BAM), *Fake-Out Boing* (funny first, then the drop), *Peek-a-boo* (squeaky hello), *Ceiling Twitch* (nervous little twitches), *Spider Disco* (party lights and moves). The motion sensor picks from *The Classic Drop*, *Creeping Doom*, *Fake-Out Boing*.

**WLED presets:** 1 Ambient, 2 Lightning, 3 Blood red, 4 Party.

**Sounds (SD folder 01):** 1 Hiss, 2 Scream, 3 Evil laugh, 4 Creaky rope, 5 Heartbeat, 6 BOING, 7 Spider Disco, 8 "Hiii!".

## Christmas: Rappelling Santa

Santa rappels down from the corner, gets stuck in a pretend chimney, and wiggles free. The eye LEDs become his twinkling eyes and red nose.

**Build:** Small Santa ornament or plush with a harness; Optional: a cardboard chimney ring around the line; WLED: warm twinkle, white sparkle, red/green chase.

**Shows:** *Ho Ho Drop* (Santa abseils in), *Stuck in the Chimney* (too many cookies), *Naughty or Nice?* (peeks at the list), *Sleigh Bell Tease* (jingles in the dark), *Jingle Disco* (Santa's got moves). The motion sensor picks from *Ho Ho Drop*, *Stuck in the Chimney*, *Naughty or Nice?*.

**WLED presets:** 11 Warm twinkle, 12 Sparkle, 13 Candy cane, 14 Party.

**Sounds (SD folder 02):** 1 Grumble, 2 HO HO HO!, 3 Jolly laugh, 4 Chimney creak, 5 Sleigh bells, 6 BOING, 7 Jingle Bell Rock, 8 "Naughty or nice?".

## Easter: Bungee Bunny

A bunny on a bungee: it drops, bounces with shrinking hops, giggles and hops back up. Kid-friendly, no scares.

**Build:** Plush bunny with a little harness; Pink LEDs for the eyes; Hang a small basket under it for Egg Delivery.

**Shows:** *Bungee Bunny* (boing boing boing), *Egg Delivery* (lowers a basket of eggs), *Peek-a-bunny* (ears first), *Hop Disco* (the bunny hop). The motion sensor picks from *Bungee Bunny*, *Egg Delivery*, *Peek-a-bunny*.

**WLED presets:** 21 Pastel breathe, 22 Pastel flash, 23 Spring green, 24 Rainbow.

**Sounds (SD folder 03):** 1 Carrot crunch, 2 Surprise honk, 3 Bunny giggle, 4 Spring sproing, 5 Ticking clock, 6 BOING, 7 Hop Hop Hooray, 8 "Happy Easter!".

## Fall: Acorn Heist Squirrel

A squirrel lives in the corner and steals acorns from your snack table. The piezo chitter is its angry chatter. Works for any autumn party, Thanksgiving included.

**Build:** Plush squirrel holding a real acorn; Amber LEDs for the eyes; Fake leaves glued around the corner; WLED: amber breathe, fire flicker.

**Shows:** *Acorn Heist* (zip down, grab, zip up), *Leaf Storm* (the corner rustles), *The Stare-Down* (won't blink first), *Nutty Shuffle* (dance break). The motion sensor picks from *Acorn Heist*, *The Stare-Down*, *Leaf Storm*.

**WLED presets:** 31 Amber glow, 32 Leaf flicker, 33 Harvest, 34 Party.

**Sounds (SD folder 04):** 1 Angry chatter, 2 Squirrel shriek, 3 Cheeky giggle, 4 Branch creak, 5 Wind gust, 6 BOING, 7 Nutty Shuffle, 8 "Mine! All mine!".

## Winter: Brain Freeze Yeti

A fluffy yeti with icy blue eyes. Scary drop with a roar, or a funny shivering Brain Freeze. The WLED aurora preset makes the room feel like the Arctic.

**Build:** White faux-fur yeti (a fluffy white plush works); Ice-blue LEDs for the eyes; WLED: icy breathe, blizzard flash, aurora.

**Shows:** *Yeti Drop* (a roar from the ceiling), *Brain Freeze* (shivering, teeth chattering), *Thin Ice* (creak... crack... DROP), *Blizzard Boogie* (the yeti can dance). The motion sensor picks from *Yeti Drop*, *Thin Ice*, *Brain Freeze*.

**WLED presets:** 41 Icy blue, 42 Blizzard, 43 Aurora, 44 Party.

**Sounds (SD folder 05):** 1 Shiver, 2 YETI ROAR, 3 Frosty laugh, 4 Ice crack, 5 Howling wind, 6 BOING, 7 Blizzard Boogie, 8 "Brrrr!".
