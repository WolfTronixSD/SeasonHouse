Timings are the firmware's real speeds (2000 steps/s drop on a 30 mm spool). The motion cooldown is shortened from 20 s to 8 s and party mode fires every 20 to 40 s instead of 1 to 4 minutes, so you don't wait around. The sounds are the actual MP3 files for the SD card (real recordings, voice lines and music, credits in the repo), played one at a time like the DFPlayer does; the clicks are the piezo chitter. Guests come in through the door; the hall light behind them shows who they are. Flashing and shaking are toned down if your device asks for reduced motion.
