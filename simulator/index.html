Timings are the firmware's real speeds (2000 steps/s drop on a 30 mm spool). The motion cooldown is shortened from 20 s to 8 s and party mode fires every 20 to 40 s instead of 1 to 4 minutes, so you don't wait around. Sounds are stand-in synth effects; the captions show which MP3 track would play. Flashing is toned down if your device asks for reduced motion.
